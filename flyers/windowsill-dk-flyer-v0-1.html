WSL Windowsill Not a gardening oracle

It gives a first reading, not a guarantee.

Windowsill uses climate history, direction, season and plant-library limits. It does not see your exact shade, room temperature, soil, watering, pests or local microclimate.

Know plants?

Help correct one plant entry. Tell us what is wrong, too optimistic, too cautious or still uncertain. The goal is correction, not endorsement.

Build with the API.

Small non-commercial, educational, local and experimental projects can ask for free API access. Rate limits protect service stability.
